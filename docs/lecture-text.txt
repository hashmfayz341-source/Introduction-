Cell – intro recall
 ◦ Basic unit of structure and function
 ◦ To maintain their structure and function cells have “homeostasis”
    ◦ Homeostasis is a dynamic condition in which cells try to maintain their internal environment
      within narrow range of physiologic parameters

 ◦ Three main types depending on activity in cell cycle(mitotic ability)
    ◦ Labile- continuous growth without stimulus
    ◦ Stable- growth with stimulus
    ◦ Permanent- no growth even with stimulus
 ◦ Every cell type has different ability to withstand the stress and its
   own homeostatic limits according to tissue function

Cell stress and injury
◦ In its specific microenvironment under physical conditions cells survive
  via homeostasis
◦ A response from the cellular mechanisms to the stress, such that
  homeostatic mechanisms were challenged to the limits


◦ Reponses depends upon
  ◦ Type of cell
  ◦ Duration and Intensity of stressor
Cell Injury- affect of the stress on the cell

If confronted with stress that endangers the structural or functional integrity of
the cell, the cell may undergo changes to survive depending upon:


◦ Cell type (Labile/ Stable/ Permanent)
◦ Duration (removed- mins/hrs/days/years)
◦ Intensity (mild/ moderate/severe)
Difference between stress and injury


◦ They are cause (stress) and effect (injury).


  ◦ Removable stressor-removed stress- reversible injury
  ◦ Persistent stressor-sustained stress- Irreversible injury
              ◦ Reversible cell injury:
                 ◦ Within certain limits, functional & morphological
                   (gross and microscopic) changes are reversible if
                 ◦ damaging stimulus is removed and cell returns to
                   stable state.
                 ◦ No membrane damage & nucleus is intact

              ◦ Irreversible cell injury (Cell Death):
Cell Injury      ◦ If stress is severe, persistent and rapid then cell
                   unable to restore normal function and progress
                   eventually to cell death.

                ◦ Cell Death either:
                   ◦ Necrosis
                   ◦ Apoptosis

6. Damage to DNA and Proteins
◦ Cells can repair minor DNA damages
◦ If the damage is severe that cannot be corrected, the cell initiates its suicide program and die
  by apoptosis.


◦ Causes of DNA damage:
  ◦ Radiation and chemical injury
  ◦ Reactive oxygen species
